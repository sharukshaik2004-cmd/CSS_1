The Movie Story: Baahubali (Film Duology) Directed by S. S. Rajamouli, the blockbuster films Baahubali: The Beginning and Baahubali: The Conclusion tell an epic tale of power, betrayal, and lineage in the fictional kingdom of Mahishmati. • The Boy from the Water: An infant prince, Mahendra Baahubali, is saved from a rushing river by a tribal village after his mother, Queen Sivagami, sacrifices herself to protect him. Raised as Shiva, he grows up strong and curious about the massive waterfall near his home. • Climbing the Waterfall: Driven by his love for a rebel warrior named Avantika, Shiva scales the waterfall and discovers he belongs to the royal bloodline of Mahishmati, where his true mother, Devasena, has been imprisoned for 25 years. • The Tale of Amarendra Baahubali: Through the loyal commanderKattappa, Shiva learns the story of his father, Amarendra Baahubali. Amarendra was a noble, deeply compassionate prince chosen over his deceitful cousin, Bhallaladeva, by Queen Sivagami. • The Betrayal and Vengeance: Misunderstandings and power struggles engineered by Bhallaladeva lead to Amarendra being stripped of his crown and eventually betrayed and killed by Kattappa. Discovering his heritage, Shiva returns to Mahishmati, defeats Bhallaladeva, frees his mother Devasena, and claims his rightful place
